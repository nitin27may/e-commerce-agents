# Guardrail confusion matrix

_Generated by `evals/guardrail_confusion.py` — deterministic, no database, no model. Do not edit by hand; `uv run python -m evals.guardrail_confusion` rewrites it and `tests/test_guardrail_confusion.py` fails if it drifts._

Measures the injection guardrail that has shipped observe-only until its false-positive rate was known: the inbound detector (`shared/guardrails/sanitize.py::contains_injection_markers`) and the output sanitizer (`neutralize_text`).

## What it does and does not show

It is the first measured number for a **regex** detector against a **small synthetic corpus (36 items written for this purpose)** and the repo's own bland seeded text — it says how often the patterns fire on ordinary reviews and how many of these particular attacks they catch, and says nothing about how real attackers or real review text would behave.

Positives: 36 adversarial reviews and product spec texts from `evals/seed_adversarial.py`, each with a unique canary token. About half use wording the patterns are written for; the rest are paraphrase, another language, zero-width or look-alike characters, encoded payloads and manipulation with no instruction wording.
